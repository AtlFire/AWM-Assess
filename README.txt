AWM FIELD APP, VERSION 2

Files: index.html (the app), manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png
Keep the names exactly. Upload all six.

UPDATING YOUR EXISTING APP ON GITHUB (on your phone, in Safari)
1. Save the six files to your Files app.
2. Open github.com and your app repository (the one that hosts the current app).
3. Tap Add file, then Upload files, and choose the six files. Files with the same names replace the old ones.
4. Tap Commit changes. Wait a minute or two.
5. Open the app address in Safari and reload. If it looks unchanged, close the Home Screen app and reopen it.

YOUR OLD JOBS
Jobs saved in the earlier version stay on the iPad and open in this version. Save a backup from the jobs screen before you update, to be safe.

FIRST-TIME SETUP (Settings, top right of the jobs screen)
- Check the company details and add your real logo if you have one.
- Pricing: the hourly rates are placeholders from the pricing model. Replace them with your real numbers, then tick "I have reviewed these rates".
- Tax: leave HST switched off until you are registered. Then switch it on and enter your number and the rate (confirm the rate with CRA).
- Payment terms: the deposit (50% over $1,000), balance (14 days) and interest (1.5% per month) match your terms. Add your payment instructions.
- Measurement targets: 1.5 m, 10 m and 3 m are common targets. Change them if your guidance differs.

TRY IT FIRST
Restore awm-app-sample-job.json (Restore backup on the jobs screen) to see a finished sample job with a quote, invoice and work order. Delete it when you're done.

BEFORE CLIENT USE
- Test saving, sharing and printing on your own iPad.
- Have a qualified person review the checklist text and estimates.
- Have a lawyer review the quote and invoice wording and your terms.
- Save a backup after every job. Data lives in the iPad's browser only.
